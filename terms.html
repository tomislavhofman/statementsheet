The service

StatementSheet reads bank statement files (ISO 20022 camt.052, camt.053, camt.054 and SWIFT MT940/MT942) in your browser and presents their contents as tables and spreadsheet exports. It is a convenience tool. The statement issued by your bank remains the authoritative record. Always check exported figures against the original before using them for bookkeeping or tax filings. StatementSheet is not tax, legal or accounting advice.

Free and Pro
